Perfume kitni der tak lasting hai?

Hamare Eau de Parfum high oil concentration ke saath bane hain — aam tor par skin par 8–12 ghante aur kapdon par us se bhi zyada lasting milti hai, mausam aur skin type ke hisaab se.

Delivery kitne din mein hoti hai? COD hai?

Pakistan bhar mein 2–4 working days mein delivery hoti hai. Cash on Delivery available hai. Rs 10,000 se zyada ke orders par delivery bilkul FREE hai, warna Rs 250 flat charges hain.

Kya main perfume wapas ya exchange kar sakta/sakti hoon?

G haan — agar bottle sealed hai to 7 din ke andar easy exchange available hai. Damage ya galat product milne par hum foran replacement bhejte hain.

50ml aur 100ml mein kya farq hai?

Khushboo dono mein same hai, sirf quantity ka farq hai. 100ml zyada value deta hai aur rozana use ke liye best hai; 50ml travel aur pehli dafa try karne ke liye perfect hai.

Original hai? Skin ke liye safe hai?

JANI ke tamam perfumes hamari apni lab mein premium, skin-safe ingredients se bante hain aur dermatologically tested hain. Sensitive skin par pehle patch test ki salah di jati hai.
